# Glossary

- **Lane**: an origin and destination pair we publish freight rates for, such as `SIN-RTM`.
- **Contract rate**: an exchange rate fixed in the annual carrier contract.
- **Spot market**: in chartering, the market for single voyages rather than time charters. `chartering.market` keeps an index of it.
- **Spot berth**: a berth booked without a terminal contract, charged at the spot rate for its class and a premium.
- **Business date**: the date on a document that decides which prices and rates apply.
