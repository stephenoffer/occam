# Coffee, tea and snacks

Every kitchen has a bean-to-cup machine, a kettle and a range of teas. Fruit is delivered on
Monday and Thursday mornings, and snacks are restocked on Wednesdays.

If you'd rather have a proper flat white, the cafe across the road on Rivington Lane gives 10%
off when you show your badge. Receipts for coffee with candidates or customers can be claimed as
expenses; your own daily coffee can't.

Running low on something? Post in #office-requests rather than messaging facilities directly.
