# Expenses

Claim expenses in the finance tool within 60 days of spending the money. Attach a receipt for
anything over £10; for smaller amounts a note of what it was for is enough.

What you can claim:

- travel booked through the travel desk, and taxis after 21:00 or when carrying equipment
- meals while travelling, up to £35 a day in the UK and the local equivalent abroad
- coffee or lunch with candidates and customers
- books and courses agreed with your manager beforehand

What you can't claim: alcohol on your own, upgrades to first or business class, fines, and
anything already paid for by a client.

Expense claims are paid with the next payroll run after your manager approves them.
