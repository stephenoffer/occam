# Booking travel

Book flights, trains and hotels through the travel desk at least two weeks ahead where you can.
The travel desk holds the company's negotiated hotel rates in London, Zürich and Berlin.

Take the train for journeys under five hours; between London and Paris or Brussels, always take
the train. Economy class for flights under eight hours, premium economy above that.

If plans change, cancel through the travel desk too, so refunds come back to the company card.
