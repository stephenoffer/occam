# Visas and work trips abroad

Before any work trip abroad, check with people-ops whether you need a visa or a work permit. Most
short business visits are fine without one, but giving training or doing hands-on work for a
client often isn't.

Allow six weeks for visa applications. The company pays the fees and any courier costs, and
people-ops can write a letter of invitation for the embassy.

Keep your passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date.
