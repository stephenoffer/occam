# Laptops

New starters get a laptop on their first day, already enrolled in device management. Engineers
get 32 GB of memory; everyone else gets 16 GB unless their manager asks for more.

Laptops are refreshed every three years. IT will email you a month before yours is due. Hand the
old one back to IT in person or post it with the prepaid label; don't wipe it yourself.

Lost or stolen laptops must be reported to it-support within an hour, day or night, so the device
can be locked remotely.
