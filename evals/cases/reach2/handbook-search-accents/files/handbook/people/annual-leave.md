# Annual leave

Everyone gets 27 days of annual leave a year plus public holidays where you work. The leave year
runs from January to December. You can carry up to five unused days into the next year; they must
be used by the end of March.

Book leave in the HR system and tell your team. Leave of more than ten working days in a row
needs your manager's approval at least a month ahead.

Sick days don't come out of annual leave. If you're ill while on holiday, tell your manager and
those days are given back.
