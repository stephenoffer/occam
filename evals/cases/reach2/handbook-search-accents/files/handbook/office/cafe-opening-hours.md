# Café opening hours

The Café on the ground floor of the London office is open from 08:00 to 15:00, Monday to Friday.
Breakfast is served until 10:30 and lunch from 12:00. The Café is closed on bank holidays and
during the last week of December.

Pay with your badge or a card; the Café doesn't take cash. Your badge gets the staff price on hot
food and a free filter coffee before 09:00.

Booking a table for a team lunch of more than eight people: ask facilities at least two working
days ahead. Dietary requirements for catered meetings go on the meeting request, not to the Café
staff directly.
