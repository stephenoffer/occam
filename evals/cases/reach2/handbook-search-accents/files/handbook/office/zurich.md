# Zürich office

Our Zürich office is on the fourth floor at Löwenstrasse 29, a five-minute walk from Zürich HB
(the main station). The door code changes on the first Monday of every month and is posted in
the #office-zrh channel.

Desks are unassigned. Book one in the desk planner before you travel; on Tuesdays and Thursdays
the office is usually full. Visitors sign in at reception on the ground floor and need a host.

The office is closed on Swiss public holidays, including Sechseläuten afternoon in April and
Knabenschiessen Monday in September.
