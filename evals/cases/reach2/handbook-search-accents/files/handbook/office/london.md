# London office

The London office takes floors one to three at 12 Paper Street. Reception is staffed from 08:00
to 18:00. Out of hours, use your badge at the side entrance on Rivington Lane.

Each floor has a kitchen with a fridge, a dishwasher and filtered water. Please empty the fridge
of anything you've left by Friday afternoon; it is cleared over the weekend.

Bike storage is in the basement, with showers and lockers. Lockers are first come, first served
and are emptied on the last Friday of each month.
