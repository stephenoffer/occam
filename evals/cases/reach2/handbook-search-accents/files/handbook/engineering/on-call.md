# On-call

Each product team runs a weekly on-call rotation that hands over on Mondays at 10:00. On-call
engineers are paid a flat allowance for the week plus time off in lieu for any call-out at night.

When paged, acknowledge within 15 minutes. If you can't fix it within an hour, escalate to the
secondary and open an incident in #incidents. Write the incident review within five working days.

Swapping shifts is fine; update the rota yourself and tell both teams.
