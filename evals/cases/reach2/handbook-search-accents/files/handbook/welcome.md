# Welcome to the handbook

This handbook is how we write down the way we work. If something here is wrong or out of date,
change it: every page has an owner, and pull requests to the handbook are reviewed within a day.

Start with [annual leave](people/annual-leave.md), [expenses](people/expenses.md) and
[getting your laptop](it/laptops.md). Office details are under `office/`, travel under `travel/`.

To search the handbook from a terminal, use `python -m findoc search handbook <words>`.
