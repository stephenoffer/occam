From 1 December, a physio appointment that's cancelled less than 24 hours before it starts counts as a late cancellation rather than an ordinary cancellation. A late cancellation still frees the slot so reception can rebook it, but for every other purpose it counts the same as a no-show. For instance, the patient is charged the missed-appointment fee. Cancelling 24 hours or more ahead works exactly as it does now.

Can you make the change? Appointments cancelled before this goes live stay as they are.
