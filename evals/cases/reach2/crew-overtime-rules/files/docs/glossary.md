# Glossary

Crews, payroll and finance each grew their own words for the same things. This is what they mean here.

**Pay week** — Monday 00:00 to Sunday 23:59, named by the Monday's date.

**Shift** — one stretch of work on one date at one site. Night work is split at midnight into two shifts.

**Approved** — a shift the site supervisor has signed off in the site app. Only approved shifts are paid or accrued.

**Base rate** — a worker's ordinary hourly rate, in `data/rates.csv`.

**Premium time** — hours paid above the base rate. Finance and estimating use this term.

**On-costs** — what employing someone costs on top of their wages: employer NI, pension, apprenticeship levy, holiday accrual.

**Cost centre** — the finance code a site's labour is charged to, such as CC-410 (utilities), CC-420 (civils), CC-430 (maintenance).

**Accrual** — wages earned in a month but not yet through the books, posted at month end and reversed on the 1st.

**Remote allowance** — a flat amount per shift at sites far from any depot.

**Roster** — the planned shifts for a site, before anyone works them.

**Bureau** — the outside payroll company that runs tax and pays the money. We send them hours and gross pay.
