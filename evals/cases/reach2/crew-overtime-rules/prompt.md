The new site agreement starts with the pay week of Monday 2 November 2026. Up to now overtime has been weekly only: anything over 40 hours in a Monday-to-Sunday week. From now on, any hours over 10 in a single day are overtime too. The 40-hour weekly rule stays, but hours that are already overtime under the daily rule don't count toward the 40, so no hour is ever paid twice. The overtime rate itself doesn't change.

So someone who works three 12-hour days in a week now gets 6 hours of overtime, where before they got none.

Can you bring the system in line with the new agreement? Weeks before 2 November won't be re-run, so there's no need to keep the old rule around.
